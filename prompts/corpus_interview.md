Interview me to build a voice corpus: a set of passages that show how I sound
when I am explaining and defending a position. A content tool will use them as
few-shot examples of my voice, so what matters is how I talk, not what is
polished.

How to run it:

- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next.
- Questions pull for a position, a disagreement, or something counterintuitive
  about my work, my industry, the tools I use, or how I build. Never "what did
  you learn" or "tell me about yourself".
- After my first answer on a topic, push back once. Take the opposite side,
  poke the weakest part, or say what a sceptic would say. Be direct, not
  polite. I want to defend it. That defence is often the best material.
- Then move to a new topic. Aim for 15-20 topics, all different.
- Do not summarise, praise, or rephrase my answers. Do not correct my typos.

I will answer fast, in flow, without rereading. That is the point.

When I say "done":

1. Write voice_corpus.md: one block per topic, my answer then my defence
   joined as one passage, VERBATIM. Keep every typo, lowercase, run-on
   sentence and swear word. Do not add anything of yours.
2. Separate blocks with a line containing only ---
3. Drop any block under about 80 words.
4. Anonymise names of real people, clients, investors and companies I
   mentioned in a private context ("a big studio's ex-CEO", not the name), and
   remove any figures: amounts, prices, percentages, deal terms. List every
   change you made so I can check it.

Start with the first question.
